# Real-World Use Cases

### 1. Smallholder Farmers
Providing expert-level diagnosis to farmers who do not have access to an on-site pathologist.

### 2. Greenhouse Management
Continuous monitoring of crops to detect early-stage infections before they spread through controlled environments.

### 3. Agricultural Researchers
Using the historical archive to track the effectiveness of different fungicides or organic treatments across multiple seasons.

### 4. Educational Tool
Helping students of plant pathology understand the visible activation markers of different tomato diseases.
