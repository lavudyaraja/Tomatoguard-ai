# Feature Ecosystem

### 1. Dual-Ensemble Classification
Uses both Vision Transformers (MaxViT) and CNNs (CoAtNet) to achieve superior generalization across different lighting and leaf conditions.

### 2. Forensic Cross-Viewer
Side-by-side comparison of the original leaf tissue with the AI's activation heatmap.

### 3. Patient History Archive
A comprehensive, searchable ledger of all past scans, allowing for seasonal trend analysis.

### 4. Interactive Pathology Hotspots
Pins located on the high-intensity areas of the leaf that describe specific symptoms identified by the computer vision model.
