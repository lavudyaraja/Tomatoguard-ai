# The Solution: Forensic Pathological AI

TomatoGuard AI provides an end-to-end diagnostic pipeline that combines deep learning classification with multimodal explanation.

### Our Approach:
- **Instant Recognition**: Upload a photo and get a diagnosis in < 5 seconds.
- **Forensic Evidence**: Grad-CAM heatmaps show exactly *where* the model sees the disease.
- **Clinical Reports**: Automated LLM-generated summaries explain the "why" and "how".
- **Actionable Guidance**: Every diagnosis comes with tailored biosecurity and recovery protocols.
